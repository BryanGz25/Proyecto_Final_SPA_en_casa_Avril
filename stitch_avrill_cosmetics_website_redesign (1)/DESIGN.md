## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory intimacy of artisanal skincare, botanical purity, and slow-living luxury. Designed for an audience that values conscious self-care, holistic wellness, and handcrafted personal rituals, the interface balances editorial sophistication with warm organic tactile warmth. 

The aesthetic is **Artisanal Minimalist Editorial**: generous negative space, gentle linen-like warm white backgrounds, soft botanical greens, and sun-warmed terracotta tones. The digital environment mirrors a high-end natural apothecary—uncluttered, serene, tactile, and mindful. Visual hierarchy avoids aggressive contrast in favor of nuanced tonal layering, refined serif typography, and graceful micro-interactions that evoke slow, intentional living.

## Colors

The color palette is derived from raw botanical matter, pressed oils, sun-dried clays, and unbleached fiber packaging.

- **Primary (`#4A5D4E`)**: Deep Sage / Botanical Olive. Anchors primary calls-to-action, key headlines, and structural navigation accents.
- **Secondary (`#C5896E`)**: Warm Terracotta / Sunbaked Earth. Serves as a tactile accent for badges, hover states, seasonal highlights, and artisanal certifications.
- **Tertiary (`#8A9A86`)**: Soft Foliage Mist. Used for secondary UI chips, botanical tag backgrounds, subtle status indicators, and delicate borders.
- **Neutral (`#2D312E`)**: Deep Charcoal. Replaces stark pitch black to deliver high legibility with an organic, ink-pressed tone.
- **Named Surfaces**:
  - `surface-canvas` (`#FDFBF7`) and `surface-subtle` (`#FAF6F0`): Warm ivory and alabaster substrates providing an unhurried, parchment-like canvas.
  - `surface-card` (`#FFFFFF`): Crisp pure white utilized strictly to isolate product vessels and soaps.
  - `border-delicate` (`#E8E2D8`): Muted sand borders establishing structure without breaking visual continuity.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical elegance with clean modern readability.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles (`Playfair Display`)**: Employs graceful proportions, high-contrast strokes, and organic serifs. Headlines celebrate formula origins, botanical aromas, and craft stories. It conveys heritage and refined indulgence.
- **Body & Product Metadata (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers friendly, clear geometry with soft terminal finishes that match botanical curves. Maintains high legibility across ingredient lists, INCI technical disclosures, usage steps, and shopping flows.
- **Uppercase Labels (`label-caps`)**: Styled in generous tracking (`0.12em`) to echo apothecary labeling conventions, vintage botanical plates, and luxury perfume packaging.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an unhurried, airy philosophy inspired by high-end coffee table publications and luxury retail spaces. 

- **Grid Structure**:
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: 12-column symmetrical layout with max-width container of `1280px`, `1.5rem` gutters, and `3rem` canvas margins. Product grids alternate comfortably between 2-column large visual showcases and 4-column catalog layouts.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer padding.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` safe margin. Product cards reflow to single-column or horizontal swipe carousels.
- **Rhythm**: Sections leverage generous vertical breathing room (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) between narrative story segments, ensuring each handcrafted formula receives distinct visual reverence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth follows an **Ambient Warm Shadow & Tonal Layering** approach. Heavy, artificial drop shadows are strictly prohibited; depth must mimic diffuse natural morning light falling across handmade soap blocks and glass droppers.

- **Base Layer**: Background canvas sits on warm ivory (`surface-canvas`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Product Tiles)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted ivory (`surface-subtle`) floating with an ultra-soft ambient shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(74, 93, 78, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 49, 46, 0.03);`
  - Paired with a delicate 1px border (`border-delicate`).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Cart Drawer, Modals, Quick-View Apothecary Sheets)**:
  - `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -4px rgba(45, 49, 46, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(74, 93, 78, 0.04);`
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Floating Action Bar / Sticky Header)**: Backdropped with light blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) at 90% opacity (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.9)`), accompanied by a hairline bottom border of `border-delicate`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded (`2`)** organic shape language. It evokes cold-process soap bars gently smoothed by water, river stones, and ceramic pinch pots.

- **Core Elements (Inputs, Buttons, Cards)**: Default to `0.5rem` (`8px`) border radius. This introduces warmth while preserving structured editorial precision.
- **Botanical Badges & Chips**: Can extend to full pill profiles (`rounded-full`) for ingredient origin tags (e.g., "Aceite de Oliva Extra Virgen", "Vegano", "Libre de Sulfatos").
- **Product Visual Containers**: Use `0.75rem` to `1rem` radius, presenting photographic packaging with a soft, tactile framed finish.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid deep olive background (`#4A5D4E`) with white text, font `label-md` weight `600`, subtle letter spacing. Padding `12px 24px`, border-radius `8px`. Hover shifts gently to a deeper foliage tone (`#3A4A3E`) with a soft ambient shadow lift.
- **Secondary / Outline Button**: Hairline border (`1.5px`) in `#4A5D4E` or `#C5896E`, transparent background, text matching border color. Hover transitions to 8% opacity wash of the stroke color.
- **Tertiary / Text Link**: Charcoal text with an understated animated underline styled in `#C5896E`, positioned 4px below baseline.

### Chips & Botanical Tags
- Compact height (`28px`), radius `14px` (pill), subtle padding (`0 12px`).
- Background in light sage tint (`rgba(138, 154, 134, 0.15)`) with `#4A5D4E` text in `label-caps` for notes like "Lavanda Silvestre", "Prensado en Frío", or "Glicerina Pura".

### Product Cards
- Elevated card container with `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF6F0` fill, 1px border in `#E8E2D8`, and Elevation 1 ambient shadow.
- Visual frame with 4:5 aspect ratio for soap bars, bath salts, and botanical oils.
- Floating upper-right corner reserved for small terracotta status badges (`#C5896E`).
- Title in `title-lg` (`Playfair Display`), botanical subtitle in `body-sm` (`#6E7570`), and bold price block in `title-md`.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Surface fill in `#FAF6F0`, border 1px solid `#E8E2D8`, border-radius `8px`, padding `12px 16px`.
- Active focus state: border shifts to `#4A5D4E` with an ultra-subtle 3px outer ring in `rgba(74, 93, 78, 0.12)`. No harsh high-contrast black rings.
- Placeholder text in `#8B8580` with typography `body-md`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 18px size, radius `4px` for checkboxes and circular for radios.
- Unchecked: `#FAF6F0` fill with `1.5px` border in `#C5896E` or `#8A9A86`.
- Checked: `#4A5D4E` solid fill with crisp ivory check icon.

### Ingredient Accordion & Botanical Spec Sheets
- Minimal horizontal hairline dividers (`#E8E2D8`).
- Accordion triggers set in `title-md` (`Playfair Display`) with delicate `+` / `−` terracotta glyphs.
- Expanded drawers expose INCI formulations, scent profiles (Top, Heart, Base notes), and ritual application tips styled in `body-md`.
